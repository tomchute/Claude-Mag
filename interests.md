## Less of this

- "Non-mainstream" that is really pseudoscience: anti-vax, "secret knowledge", miracle protocols. Frontier science yes, fringe claims no
- Hype, clickbait, "this will change your life forever"

- Generic wellness advice and reassurance pieces

- Health myths that have been debunked (e.g. "red wine is good for your heart")
- Corporate or waffly writing; prefer plain British English
- Material centred on childhood when the subject is adult life
- Paywalled teasers that cut off on Kindle (inferred)
- Self-help that's only American voices (inferred)
